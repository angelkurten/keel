# Plans, seats and proration

Relay has three plans, billed per seat and month: Starter ($8), Pro ($30) and Enterprise ($50). Annual billing is available on Pro and Enterprise with 10% off, paid upfront.

When a customer changes plan in the middle of a billing cycle, Relay issues a separate prorated invoice for the difference, covering the days left in the cycle. The monthly invoice of the next cycle is at the new price. Customers sometimes read the prorated invoice as a second charge for the same month: it is not; it only covers the upgrade for the remaining days.

Adding seats mid-cycle is prorated the same way. Removing seats takes effect at the next cycle.
