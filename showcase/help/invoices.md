# Invoices and their language

Invoices are emailed to the billing contacts on the 1st of each month and are always available under Settings > Billing. Each invoice shows the plan, the seats and the period; prorated invoices say "prorated" and the days they cover.

The invoice language follows the billing contact's language setting (English, Spanish, Portuguese, French or German). Support can change it per contact on request; the next invoice is sent in the new language.

A failed payment is retried after 3 and 7 days; the account stays active for 14 days while payment is pending.
